The **Settings** page holds what you can change from the app. Its sections
appear below in the order the page shows them.

![The Settings page with its Security, Portfolio and Agent sections](/img/app/en/settings.png)

Most rows save as soon as you change them and say **✓ Saved**. If a value is
refused, the row says **Not saved:** and the reason. Settings that are fixed when
the container starts are environment variables. See
[Configuration](./configuration.mdx).

## Security {#security}

This section holds the **Access code**, the **Auto-lock** delay and the
**Access tokens**. [Secure access](./secure-access.mdx) covers each of them
step by step.

## Portfolio {#portfolio}

### Currency {#base-currency}

The base currency is the currency every figure is reported in. You choose it at
the first launch. Once it is set, the row shows the code with **set at first
run** and cannot be changed: your amounts are recorded in that currency.

### Comparison funds {#funds}

The index funds the **Comparison** page can compare you with. **Add a fund**
opens the list of five funds, each with a switch to follow it. **Stop following**
removes a fund from the page. See [Comparison](./benchmark.mdx#follow).

### Tax models {#taxation}

Your taxation models, each with a summary of its rates and the accounts that
carry it. **Add a model** imports a published model or lets you write your own.
**Edit** opens a model. See [Your taxation model](./your-taxation-model.mdx).

## Agent {#agent}

This section turns the **Agent** page on, lists the **Providers** that run the
model and sets its **Thinking** time. [Set up the agent](./set-up-the-agent.mdx)
explains each row, and [Agent](./agent.mdx) explains how to use it.

## Notifications {#notifications}

A channel sends you a message outside the app when something needs your
attention.

1. Click **Add a channel**.
2. Choose the **Channel kind**: **Webhook**, **ntfy**, **Apprise** or
   **Email**.
3. Fill in the address. For example, an ntfy **Topic address** looks like
   `https://ntfy.sh/my-topic`. An email channel also asks for the **SMTP
   server**, **Port**, **Username**, **Password**, **Sender** and **STARTTLS**.
4. Under **Send**, pick at least one kind of message: **Late prices**, **New
   versions**, **Errors**.
5. Click **Save**.

**Test** on the channel's row sends a test message. In the channel's sheet,
**Channel on** pauses it and **Delete this channel** removes it.

| Kind of message | When it is sent |
|---|---|
| **Late prices** | A security you hold has a market open, but its price has not changed for longer than the **Frozen-quote threshold** of [Advanced](#advanced). Sent again only after the price recovers and stops again. |
| **New versions** | A newer release of SuiviBourse is out. The check runs only while **Report new versions** is on, on the **System** page. |
| **Errors** | A background job fails: price updates, history rebuild or performance calculation. At most once every 6 hours per job. |

## Display {#display}

These rows apply to the browser you are using. Another browser keeps its own
choice.

| Row | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Appearance** | **Automatic** (follows your system), **Light**, **Dark** |
| **Language** | **Français**, **English** |
| **Table density** | **Comfortable**, **Compact** |
| **Theme** | Says **Atelier** (the built-in theme) or **Custom**. **Customise** opens the theme editor. |

### Edit the theme {#theme}

The theme editor, at `/settings/theme`, changes the colours, shape and fonts of
the app. The theme is saved on the server, so every browser gets it.

![The theme editor: the colour list on the left and a preview of a page on the right](/img/app/en/settings-theme.png)

1. On **Display → Theme**, click **Customise**.
2. Under **Palette to edit**, choose **Light** or **Dark**.
3. Change the colours in **Grounds and text**, **Ink and signs** and **Charts**.
   A dot marks a changed colour, and **Reset** on its row brings it back.
4. Adjust **Shape** (**Corner radius**, **Shadows**, **Letter spacing**) and
   **Fonts** (**Text**, **Figures and identifiers**).
5. Check the preview on the right. You can switch the page it shows.
6. Click **Apply** to save.

If you leave with unapplied changes, the app asks: **Stay** or **Discard**.

At the top of the editor:

- **Import from tweakcn** loads a theme from tweakcn.com. Enter its id or the
  address of its page, for example `tweakcn.com/themes/…`, then click
  **Import**. The server contacts tweakcn.com once, on that click.
- **Edit the JSON** opens the theme as JSON. **Apply** in that window saves it
  at once.
- **Export** downloads the current theme as `theme.json`.

**Restore Atelier**, at the bottom of the editor, brings back the built-in
theme.

## Advanced {#advanced}

These rows control how often the app collects prices and recomputes your
figures. Each row shows its default and a **Reset** link when you changed it.

| Row | What it sets | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Quote frequency** | How often prices refresh while markets are open | 2 min |
| **Recalculation frequency** | How often the history rebuild runs. The performance calculation runs every 2 minutes, whatever this value. | 1 min |
| **Pause between requests** | The wait between two calls to the price source | 10 sec |
| **History depth** | Days of prices loaded per request when history is fetched | 365 d |
| **Frozen-quote threshold** | How long an unchanged price can last before the **System** page flags it. 0 turns the check off. | 15 min |

[How prices are collected](./how-prices-are-collected.mdx) explains what these
jobs do.

## Search with ⌘K {#palette}

Press ⌘K, or Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux, to open the search palette from any
page. Type to reach a security, an account, an event, an agent thread or a page,
or to run an action: **Record an event**, **Import a statement**, **Add an
account**, **New thread with the agent** or **Lock**.

![The search palette open over the dashboard, listing securities, accounts and actions](/img/app/en/palette.png)
