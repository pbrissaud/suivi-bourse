On the **Agent** page you ask questions about your portfolio in plain language.
The agent reads your lines, accounts and ledger, simulates a sale and its tax,
and proposes events for your ledger. It does not give advice.

![The Agent page: the threads on the left, a question, the tool lines and the answer](/img/app/en/agent.png)

The agent needs a model connected first. See
[Set up the agent](./set-up-the-agent.mdx). Until then the page shows **Connect a
model to ask questions about your portfolio** and a **Configure** button.

## Ask a question {#ask}

1. Open **Agent** in the sidebar.
2. Type your question in **Ask a question about your portfolio…**, or click one
   of the example questions.
3. Press Enter or click **Send**. Shift+Enter starts a new line.

Click **Stop** to interrupt the answer, or **Retry** if it stops on an error.

| You can ask | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Where does my portfolio stand today? | Reads your totals and lines |
| What are my latest movements? | Reads your ledger |
| Have I invested regularly over the last twelve months? | Reads your investment rhythm |
| If I sell all my ACME on my PEA, how much do I pay and what is left? | Simulates the sale and its tax |
| What would emptying my brokerage account cost today? | Simulates selling the account's lines |
| Am I doing better than my comparison fund? | Reads the **Comparison** page |
| How has my portfolio moved since January? | Reads your portfolio history |

## See which tools it used {#tools}

Each step of the agent appears as a line in the answer, for example **Reading
your ledger.** or **Simulating a sale and its tax.** Click a line to see the
**Call** and the **Result**. A step that fails says **The tool failed:** and the
reason.

## Add the events it proposes {#events}

When you describe movements, or give it a statement, the agent proposes events.
They appear under the answer as a table: date, type, event and amount. A line
that is already in your ledger says **already in the ledger** and is
unchecked.

1. Check the lines you want to keep.
2. Click **Add 3 events**. The count follows your selection.

When a proposal needs your answers, the button reads **Review in the ledger**
instead. See [Review a proposal in the ledger](#review).

Nothing is written before you click. The card then shows one of these states:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Added on …** | The events are in your ledger. |
| **Ignored** | You clicked **Ignore**. |
| **Expired** | Nobody decided within 7 days. |
| **The ledger has changed since this proposal** | Click **Run again** to check the same events against your ledger as it is now. |

## Review a proposal in the ledger {#review}

**Review in the ledger** opens the **Ledger** with a window titled **What this
import would do**. It lists **The proposed events** and asks the questions the
proposal leaves open. Nothing is written until you click **Import**.

| Part of the window | What you decide |
|---|---|
| **The accounts** | For each account the events name, choose its target in **Target for …**: one of your accounts, or **Add "…" as a new account**. The choice applies to this import only. |
| **Amounts in another currency** | For securities quoted outside your base currency, say whether the amounts are **In EUR, already converted** or in each security's own currency. The second choice converts at the closing rate of each trade's day. |
| **Repeated in the file** | Lines that appear more than once. Choose **Write just one** or write every copy, line by line or with **One of each** and **Write every copy**. |
| **Already in your ledger** | Lines your ledger already holds. They are skipped unless you check **Write the lines already in your ledger anyway**. |
| **The currency** | Shown when you have no base currency yet. **Use … as the base currency** adopts the currency of the events. You cannot change it afterwards. |

**Import** stays disabled while an answer is missing, and the window says which.
Closing the window writes nothing and leaves the proposal waiting.

## Turn a broker statement into events {#statement}

1. Under the question box, click **Attach a statement**.
2. In **Statement account**, choose the account the statement belongs to.
3. Paste the statement in **Statement text**, up to 50 000 characters.
4. Click **Send**.

You can also drop a `.txt` or `.csv` file into the question box to fill the
window. The window names the provider and model that will read the statement.

The agent answers with proposed events. A line can carry a mark: **taken from
the statement**, **inferred from a gap** or **estimated price**.

If the agent read the statement the wrong way, click **Read as an operations
statement** or **Read as a lines statement**. Then click
[Review in the ledger](#review).

The agent reads text only. An image or a PDF is refused with **This model does
not read images. Paste the text or a CSV.**

## Your investor profile {#profile}

During a conversation the agent can propose to remember a fact about you. The
card reads **Remember for later:** and the fact, with **Confirm** and
**Ignore**.

| Fact | Values |
|---|---|
| Annual income | An amount in your base currency |
| Marginal tax rate | A percentage, 60 % at most |
| Risk tolerance | **Cautious**, **Balanced** or **Dynamic** |
| Project | A name, a target year and an optional amount |

Confirmed facts appear under **Your profile**, each with **Edit** and
**Forget**. The agent uses them in later answers. Your income amount is never
sent to the model: the agent only knows that you declared one.

## Threads {#threads}

Each conversation is a thread, kept on your server.

- **New thread** starts a new conversation.
- The list on the left shows your threads by day. Type in **Search threads**
  to find one.
- **Delete**, next to the thread's title, removes the open thread after you
  confirm.
- The model name in the question box opens the list of models. Pick another
  one for this thread.

The ⌘K palette also lists your threads.

## Usage statistics {#stats}

Click **Statistics** in the threads list to see how much the agent has used.

![The agent statistics page: tokens exchanged, threads, turns, tool calls and the weekly chart](/img/app/en/agent-stats.png)

Choose the **Period**: **1 month**, **This year**, **1 year** or **All**.

| Figure | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Tokens exchanged** | All tokens sent and received over the period |
| **Threads**, **Turns**, **Tool calls** | Conversations, answers and tool steps |
| **Input read from the cache**, **New input**, **Output** | The three kinds of tokens |
| **By model**, **By thread** | The same counts per model and per thread |

## What it cannot do {#limits}

- It writes nothing by itself. Events and profile facts are proposals that you
  confirm.
- It does not give advice. It works from your data and simulates.
- It cannot change your settings, accounts or taxation models.
- It reads text only.

When the agent answers, your lines, accounts and ledger are sent to the
provider of the model. With a model on your own network, they stay there.
