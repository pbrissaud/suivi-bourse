title: Your taxation model
description: Create a taxation model, attach it to an account, and see what you would owe if you sold today
---

A taxation model describes how one account is taxed. With a model attached, the
account page shows **If I sell today**: the tax you would owe if you sold
everything today. See [Estimated tax](./read-your-figures.mdx#projected-tax).

![The Tax models list in Settings, with three models and the accounts that carry them](/img/app/en/settings-taxation.png)

The app ships no tax rates: you import a model published on this site, or you
write your own.

## Create a model and attach it {#where}

One model can serve several accounts.

1. Open **Settings** and go to **Portfolio → Tax models**.
2. Click **Add a model**.
3. Under **Source**, choose **Write my own**.
4. Fill in **What to call this model** and choose a **Shape**.
5. Fill in the fields of that shape. See [The shapes](#shapes).
6. Click **Save**.

To attach the model to an account:

1. Open **Accounts** and select the account.
2. Click **Edit this account**.
3. In **Taxation model**, choose the model.
4. If the model counts the age from the opening day, enter the **Opened on**
   date.
5. Click **Save**.

To change a model, click **Edit** on its row. The change applies to every
account listed under **Accounts that carry it**. To remove a model, open it and
click **Delete this model**. The app refuses while an account carries it.

## Import a model for your country {#import}

1. In **Tax models**, click **Add a model**.
2. Under **Source**, choose **Import for my country**.
3. The list opens on your country. Use **Search for a wrapper** for another.
4. Click **Import** on the wrapper you want.

The model appears in **Tax models** under the wrapper's name. Open it with
**Edit** to check the values. The copy never updates by itself.

A wrapper whose name you already use shows **already imported**. If the list
cannot be read, the sheet offers **Try again**.
[Taxation models in Europe](./taxation-models-in-europe.mdx) describes the same
wrappers.

## The shapes {#shapes}

A model has one of five shapes. You type rates as percentages: `12.8`, not
`0.128`. Bracket ceilings are amounts in your base currency.

| Shape | What it taxes | Fields | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Not taxed** | Nothing | None | A tax-exempt account |
| **One rate on the gain** | The gain, when you sell | **Rate**, optional **Social charges** | Rate 25, social charges 5: a gain of 1 000 owes 300 |
| **A rate that changes with the wrapper's age** | The whole gain of the wrapper (value minus net contributed), when money leaves it, at one rate before a threshold and another after | **Rate before the threshold**, **Rate after the threshold**, **Threshold, in years**, **Counted from**, optional **Social charges** | 20 before, 0 after 5 years, social charges 10: a gain of 1 000 owes 300 in year 3 and 100 in year 6 |
| **Brackets on the gain** | The gain, slice by slice | **Brackets**: each row has **Up to** and **Rate for this bracket**; the last row is **And above**. Optional **Social charges** | Up to 6 000 at 19, up to 50 000 at 21, above at 23: a gain of 10 000 owes 1 980 |
| **A withholding on income** | What you receive, such as a dividend | **Rate** | Rate 30 on dividends; no tax is estimated on a sale |

Social charges are added to the rate, before and after an age threshold. Edit a
ladder with **Add a bracket** and **Remove this bracket**.

## What the age is counted from {#age-basis}

The shape **A rate that changes with the wrapper's age** asks **Counted from**:

| Choice | Date used |
|---|---|
| **The day the wrapper was opened** | The **Opened on** date of the account. Without that date, the account shows no estimate. |
| **The first payment into it** | The date of the account's first deposit. If the account has no deposit yet, the **Opened on** date. |

The rate changes on the anniversary itself.

Until you save a date, **Opened on** shows the account's first deposit as a
suggestion. A model counted from the opening day needs a date you saved.

## What the app refuses {#refusals}

If a value is refused when you save, the sheet stays open with the message and
nothing is saved.

| What you entered | The rule |
|---|---|
| A required field left empty | Only **Social charges** is optional |
| A rate below 0 % or above 100 % | A rate is between 0 and 100 |
| A rate plus social charges above 100 % | A gain cannot owe more than itself |
| A threshold that is negative, not a whole number, or above 200 | A whole number of years, 200 at most |
| No bracket | At least one bracket |
| Ceilings at zero or below, or that do not go up | Each ceiling is above zero and above the one before |
| A ceiling on the last bracket, or a missing ceiling on another one | Only the last bracket is open |
| Text where a number goes | Numbers only |

A model that breaks a rule added by a newer version shows no estimate: edit it
and save.
