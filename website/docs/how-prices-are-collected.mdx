This page explains where SuiviBourse gets its prices, how often it fetches
them, and what to do when a price stops updating.

## Where prices come from {#source}

Prices and exchange rates come from Yahoo Finance. The app fetches a price for
each security you hold, using the symbol written in your events, for example
`ABCD.PA` or `XYZ`. A symbol Yahoo does not know gets no price.

The app only polls securities you currently hold. When you sell a position
completely, its polling stops. Its past prices stay stored, because your
returns still need them.

## How often {#cadence}

Each security follows its own market's hours.

| Market state | What the app does |
|---|---|
| Open | Fetches the price every 2 minutes (**Quote frequency**). |
| After the close | Fetches again every 5 minutes for up to 30 minutes, to record the official closing price. |
| Closed | Waits until the market opens again, at most 24 hours. If the next opening is unknown, it tries again after 1 minute. |

Past prices are fetched by a separate task, the history rebuild. See
[Rebuild and resolution](./rebuild-and-resolution.mdx).

### Adjust the cadence {#settings}

The dials are in **Settings → Advanced**.

| Setting | Default | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Quote frequency** | 2 min | How often prices refresh while a market is open. Also the base of the retry delay below. |
| **Recalculation frequency** | 1 min | How often the history rebuild runs. |
| **Pause between requests** | 10 sec | The wait between two requests of the history rebuild. |
| **History depth** | 365 d | Days of past prices fetched per request. |
| **Frozen-quote threshold** | 15 min | How long a price may stay unchanged, market open, before the app flags it. `0` turns the check off. |

A change takes effect without a restart.

## A symbol that stops answering {#failing}

A symbol can be mistyped, delisted or renamed. When a fetch returns no price:

1. The first three failures retry at the normal cadence.
2. From the fourth, the delay doubles at each failure, up to 24 hours.
3. The first price received brings the symbol back to the normal cadence.

A closed market does not count as a failure.

While it fails, the **Quote update** row of the [System page](./system.mdx)
shows a tall bar for that hour. `/health` reports `failing`, then `backoff`,
and lists the symbol under `attention`. If no price was ever
received, the **Shares** page says **No price was received for this share**.

Until a price arrives, the holding is counted at its purchase price. See
[Read your figures](./read-your-figures.mdx).

To fix it:

1. Look the symbol up on Yahoo Finance and note the exact spelling, including
   the exchange suffix.
2. If it differs, correct the symbol in your events in the
   [Ledger](./ledger.mdx).
3. On the **System** page, press **Run** on **Quote update** to fetch at once.

## Currency conversion {#currencies}

Your figures are in your base currency. Securities are quoted in their own
currency. Each stored price keeps three values: the price in its own currency,
the price in your base currency, and the exchange rate used. A figure from
years ago is converted at the rate of its own day.

Some exchanges quote in a subunit. The app converts it for you:

| Quoted in | Read as |
|---|---|
| `GBp` (pence) | 1/100 of `GBP` |
| `ILA` (agorot) | 1/100 of `ILS` |
| `ZAc` (cents) | 1/100 of `ZAR` |

When a rate is missing, the price is still stored, and the figure says
**waiting for a rate**. The history rebuild fills in the rate later. If no rate
exists at all for a currency, the **History rebuild** task shows **Needs a
look** and `/health` reports `unconvertible`.

Until you choose a base currency, prices are stored in their own currency only
and no performance is computed. Once you choose it, every stored price is
converted.

## What a frozen price looks like {#frozen}

The **Frozen-quote threshold** drives two signals.

| Where | What it says | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Bell | **The price of** a security **has not moved since** a time | Market open, but no new price was stored within the threshold. The value shown may be slightly behind. |
| **System** page, followed securities | **frozen since** a time, with a number of passes | The stored price has stayed the same for longer than the threshold while the live price moves. `/health` reports `frozen`. |

The first usually clears at the next pass. The second means prices are fetched
but the stored one does not change. It does not stop polling.

If a signal stays:

1. Open the **Journal** from the **System** page, and choose **Warnings**.
2. Search for the symbol.
3. Press **Run** on **Quote update**.
