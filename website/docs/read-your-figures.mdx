This page will carry one anchor per figure the app explains, so that the
information icon beside a number links to the rule that number rests on.

:::info Not written yet
It is the subject of its own ticket. What it will hold, one stable anchor each:

- the unit cost basis, and why fees are absorbed into it;
- the total gain and its three terms — latent, realized, dividends;
- TWR and XIRR, and why they answer two different questions;
- the carrying price, and the four ways a figure can be absent.

The anchors are written by hand and never derived from a heading: a reworded
title would otherwise break every convention note in the app at once, silently.
:::
