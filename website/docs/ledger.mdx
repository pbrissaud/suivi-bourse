The **Ledger** holds every event of your portfolio: purchases, sales, free
shares, dividends, and money paid in or taken out. Every figure in SuiviBourse
is computed from these events.

![The Ledger: the search and filters on the left, Import, Export and Enter an event on the right, the events below](/img/app/en/ledger.png)

The newest events come first. More rows appear as you scroll. On a phone, the
table is a list.

## The six kinds of event {#kinds}

| Kind | Code in a file | What it records | Fields |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Buy** | `BUY` | Shares you bought. | **Security**, **Quantity**, **Unit price**, **Fee** |
| **Sell** | `SELL` | Shares you sold. | **Security**, **Quantity**, **Unit price**, **Fee** |
| **Free shares** | `GRANT` | Shares you received, such as a bonus share or an employee award. | **Security**, **Quantity**, **Unit price** (optional) |
| **Dividend** | `DIVIDEND` | A dividend paid to you. | **Security**, **Amount**, **Fee** |
| **Cash in** | `DEPOSIT` | Money you paid into the account. | **Amount**, **Fee** |
| **Cash out** | `WITHDRAWAL` | Money you took out of the account. | **Amount**, **Fee** |

Every event also takes a **Date**, an **Account** and an optional **Label**.

- Enter amounts and prices in your base currency, as positive numbers. The kind
  of event gives the direction.
- On **Free shares**, the form suggests the day's closing price as **Unit
  price**, and that price counts in your cost. Clear it to record shares that
  cost you nothing, such as a bonus share.
- On a **Dividend**, the **Amount** is the gross sum and the **Fee** is what was
  withheld from it. The fee cannot exceed the amount.
- The **Fee** on **Cash in** and **Cash out** is a transfer fee.

## Record an event {#record}

![The Enter an event panel with the kind tabs, the fields of a purchase and the cash line at the bottom](/img/app/en/event-form.png)

1. Click **Enter an event**.
2. Under **What happened**, pick the kind of event. The form shows only its fields.
3. Fill in the **Date** and choose the **Account**.
4. In **Security**, type a name or a ticker.
5. Pick a line from the list.
6. Fill in the other fields.
7. Check the cash line at the bottom of the panel.
8. Click **Save**.

The **Security** list shows the securities from your ledger first, then
matches from Yahoo! Finance. If you type a ticker that neither knows, the form
says so. Pick a line from the list, or click **Record … with no known quote**
to keep the ticker as typed.

The line at the bottom says how much cash leaves or arrives on the account, and
how much of it is fees. On a sale, it also gives the gain or loss.

The form warns you when the date is in the future or before the account's
opening day. It refuses a sale of more shares than the account holds on that
date.

## Find events {#filter}

- **Search**: type part of a ticker, a security name or a label.
- **Type**: show one kind of event. **All types** removes the filter.
- **Account**: show one account. It appears when your events name more than one account.
- **Period**: show one year. **Since the start** removes the filter.

**Remove filters** clears them all.

## Correct an event {#correct}

1. Click the event.
2. Change what is wrong in the **Edit event** panel.
3. Click **Save**.

The figures are recomputed after each change.

## Delete events {#delete}

To delete one event:

1. Click the event.
2. Click **Delete this event** at the bottom of the panel.
3. Confirm with **Delete**.

To delete several events, such as a whole import:

1. Filter the ledger down to them, for example by **Period** and **Account**.
2. Tick their boxes, or tick the box in the heading to select every filtered event.
3. Click **Delete**.
4. Confirm the deletion.

:::warning
A deletion cannot be undone. Export your ledger first if you may need the events
again.
:::

A deletion is refused when a later sale depends on the shares it removes. The
message names the security.

## Export {#export}

Click **Export** and choose a file:

| Entry | What you get |
|---|---|
| **All your events** (`.csv`) | Every event, in the import format. |
| **All your events** (`.xlsx`) | The same events in a workbook, one sheet per year. |
| **The filtered selection** | The events the filters show, in the import format. |
| **Accounts and lines** | Each account with its cash, positions, average cost and value. |

The event files can be imported again. See
[Import your events](./import-your-events.mdx). An export holds your events
only: settings, taxation models and tokens are not in it. To keep everything,
see [Back up and update](./backup-and-update.mdx).

To import a file of events, click **Import**.
