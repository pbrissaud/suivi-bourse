The **Accounts** page lets you create your accounts and read each one: its
value, its gain, where the gain comes from and the tax if you sold today.

![The Accounts page: the accounts as tabs with their value, then the detail of the open account](/img/app/en/accounts.png)

Your accounts are listed across the top, each with its value. Click one to open
its detail below. **Edit this account** and **Add an account** sit on the same line.

## Add an account {#add}

![The Add an account panel with the Identifier, Label, Opened on and Taxation model fields](/img/app/en/account-form.png)

1. Click **Add an account**.
2. In **Identifier**, type a short id such as `broker-a`. Use letters, digits,
   `-`, `_` and `.`, with no space. Your import files name the account by this id.
3. In **Label**, type the name to show on screen, for example `Broker A` (optional).
4. In **Opened on**, enter the day the account was opened (optional).
5. In **Taxation model**, pick a model, or leave **No model**.
6. Click **Add**.

You cannot change the identifier afterwards. If another account already uses
it, the panel says so under the field.

If some events name no account yet, the panel offers to assign them to the new
account. The box is ticked by default.

## Edit an account {#edit}

1. Open the account.
2. Click **Edit this account**.
3. Change the **Label**, **Opened on** or **Taxation model**.
4. Click **Save**.

The panel also shows what the account carries: its events, its securities and
its value.

## Attach a taxation model {#taxation}

A taxation model lets the account show the tax due if you sold today, and feeds
**After tax if all sold** on the **Dashboard**.

1. Open the account.
2. Click **Edit this account**. On an account with no model, **Choose a model**
   under **If I sell today** opens the same panel.
3. In **Taxation model**, pick a model.
4. Click **Save**.

The list shows the models you have imported or written. To add one, see
[Your taxation model](./your-taxation-model.mdx).

## Remove an account {#remove}

1. Open the account.
2. Click **Edit this account**.
3. Click **Remove this account** at the bottom of the panel.
4. Confirm with **Delete the account**.

The removal cannot be undone. It is offered only when no event names the
account. Otherwise the panel says how many events do: move them to another
account or delete them first. See [Ledger](./ledger.mdx).

The **Unassigned** account, which every installation has, cannot be removed.
Rename it instead.

## Read an account {#read}

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Account value** | The value of the account, its gain in money and percent, and its **MWR** (money-weighted return) a year. |
| Facts on the right | **Opened on**, **Cash** and the account's **Share of the portfolio**. |
| Monthly line | How much you paid in a month on average since opening. |
| **Value against what you have paid in** | The account's chart. Choose **Amounts** or **Performance**, and a period: **1 month**, **This year**, **1 year** or **All**. |
| **Where the gain comes from** | The gain since opening, split into **Unrealised gain**, **Realised gain**, **Dividends** and **Transfer fees**. |
| **If I sell today** | The **Taxable gain**, the **Income tax**, the **Social levies** and what you would keep **net after tax**, with the **Model** used. |
| **This account's shares** | The securities held in the account, with **Invested**, **Value**, **Gain** and **Weight**. The link under it opens [Shares](./shares.mdx#account) reduced to this account. |

The figures are defined in [Read your figures](./read-your-figures.mdx).

A warning under the facts means the opening day looks wrong: it is in the
future, or later than the first payment. Correct it with **Edit this account**.

## Events with no account {#unassigned}

Events recorded before you declared any account sit in **Unassigned**. Its page
shows **Events assigned to no account**.

1. In **Assign them to**, choose an account.
2. Click **Assign these events**.
