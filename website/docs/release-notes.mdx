watch out for. The exhaustive, commit-level list — every dependency bump

This page opens at **5.0** and never looks further back. The releases of version
4 are announcements of software that no longer runs the way this documentation
describes, so their notes stay with the version 4 documentation — switch
versions with the selector in the top-right navbar. Version 3 never had
hand-written notes; its history is in `CHANGELOG.md` above.

## 5.0 — Your data moves house

**This release changes where your data lives.** Version 4 kept prices in an
InfluxDB 3 Core you ran beside the app and showed them in a Grafana you
provisioned; version 5 keeps everything in **one embedded store, inside the
container**, and draws its own pages. That is not a repackaging: it is the reason
there is **no upgrade path** from version 4, and the reason the very first thing
to get right is the volume you mount.

If you are running version 4, read [Coming from v4](./coming-from-v4.mdx) before
anything else. Nothing here is applied to an existing install.

### One container, and a store you can lose if you do not mount it

The stack is gone — no database to run beside the app, no dashboard tool to
provision, no Compose file to assemble. `docker run` with one volume is the whole
install, and `docker-compose/` has left the repository along with the `Makefile`
that built its `.env`.

A container started **without** a volume still starts, and simply keeps nothing.
It says so at every boot, in its first log lines and in the
`sb_store_ephemeral` gauge, rather than refusing: an ephemeral run is a fair way
to try the app for an evening, and the loss it risks is stated instead of
guarded. See [Get started](./get-started.mdx).

### Its own interface — and the gauges are not a legacy

The app serves four pages of its own: a dashboard, your shares, your accounts,
and the data you gave it. Every figure explains, on the figure itself, the
convention it rests on, and links to the page that states the rule in full —
[Read your figures](./read-your-figures.mdx).

Grafana retires with InfluxDB; the shipped dashboards go with it. **Prometheus
stays**, and is promoted: an install whose owner never opens a page is a
supported way to run this app, not a leftover. The inventory is
[Headless: the gauges](./headless-gauges.mdx).

### Four conventions were changed on purpose

- **One base currency**, chosen on first run and reported everywhere. Prices are
  converted at write time and each point stores the native price, the converted
  price and the rate used. The base currency has **no default**; it is the single
  question the app asks, and it stops being changeable at your first event.
- **Weighted-average cost**, with acquisition fees absorbed into the cost basis
  and disposal fees into the proceeds. There is no FIFO dial.
- **Three named gain terms** — latent, realized, dividends — replacing one
  composite. Realized gain is a *breakdown* of the total, never a term added to
  it.
- **A held position with no price is carried at its own unit cost**, so a symbol
  whose quote never arrives is neither blank nor worth zero.

### Accounts are data, settings live in the store

Accounts are declared like events are — from a file of the event format with its
provenance recorded, or from the interface — and are no longer a block of a
settings file. There is always at least one; when you have declared none it is
called `default`. See [Import your events](./import-your-events.mdx).

Every dial now lives **in the store**, turned on the
[Settings](./settings.mdx) page, with no environment form and no precedence rule.
What the environment still says is the handful of things the process must know
before it can open the store: the two directories, the two ports, the metrics
switch and the log level. Variables it no longer reads are **named at startup
and not obeyed**, in one grouped notice.

### History is rebuilt, and then aged

Past prices are fetched backwards to your first purchase or grant, forwards over
a session missed while the app was down, and sideways to repair a conversion that
had no rate. Count **about 25 minutes for 30 symbols over five years** on a fresh
install.

Points are then aged in place — as written under a year, hourly to two years,
daily beyond. The ladder is a ceiling and never a floor, it has no setting, and
fine resolution is the one thing no late backup ever recovers:
[Rebuild and resolution](./rebuild-and-resolution.mdx).

### The image is renamed

`ghcr.io/pbrissaud/suivi-bourse-app` becomes **`ghcr.io/pbrissaud/suivi-bourse`**,
on both registries, with no compatibility alias. Renaming costs nothing precisely
because nothing migrates — and it buys something: no `5` tag ever exists under
the old name, so a version 4 install that bumps its tag fails at the pull rather
than starting on a store that dies with the container.
