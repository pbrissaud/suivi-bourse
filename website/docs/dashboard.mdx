The **Dashboard** answers how much your portfolio is worth and how it got
there. This page explains each part of it and the controls of its chart.

![The dashboard: the portfolio value at the top, the chart under it, then the investment rhythm and the securities value](/img/app/en/dashboard.png)

The header shows **Prices as of** and the time of the freshest price the page uses.

## Portfolio value {#value}

The top of the page shows your **Portfolio value**: your securities at their
latest price, plus the cash left in your accounts.

| Item | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Line under the value | The change in value over the period chosen on the chart, in money and percent. **excluding deposits** means money you paid in during the period does not count as a gain. |
| **First deposit** | The date of your first deposit. |
| **Cash** | The cash left in your accounts, all accounts together. |
| **After tax if all sold** | What you would keep if you sold everything today, after the tax of the accounts that carry a taxation model. It reads **If all sold, before tax** when no account has a model. |
| Two-colour bar | How much of the value is money you paid in, and how much is gain. |
| **Net contributed** | What you paid in, minus what you took out. Free shares are counted in it and named beside it. |
| **Total P&L** | Your gain: unrealised and realised gains, plus dividends, minus fees. |
| **Money-weighted return** | Your yearly return, taking into account when you paid money in. |

Each figure is defined in [Read your figures](./read-your-figures.mdx).

If you have recorded no **Cash in** or **Cash out** events, the top reads
**Securities** and shows the value of your securities alone. Cash, net
contributed and the returns need your deposits. See [Ledger](./ledger.mdx).

## Use the chart {#chart}

The chart sits under the value. Its controls are on the bar above it.

1. Choose what the chart draws:
   - **Amounts**: your **Total value** against your **Net contributed**. The
     area between the two curves is your gain.
   - **Performance**: the growth of the whole portfolio, cash included, which
     deposits and withdrawals do not move.
2. Choose the period: **1 month**, **This year**, **1 year** or **All**. The
   page opens on **1 year**.
3. Point at the chart, or focus it and use the arrow keys, to read the values of
   one day.

The range also sets the period of the change line under the value.

Without deposits, the chart offers **Amounts** only. It then draws the
**Market value** of your securities against their **Cost**.

## Investment rhythm {#rhythm}

**Investment rhythm** shows how much you buy each month. The large figure is the
median amount bought a month. The bars show each month of the period, and a
month with no purchase sits on the baseline. The line under the figure says how
much you invested over how many months, and which months had no purchase.

## Securities value {#securities}

**Securities value** shows where the value of your securities sits, by security.
The largest holdings are listed with their share of the total. The rest are
grouped as other holdings. Cash is left out.

To see every line, go to [Shares](./shares.mdx).

## Messages you may see {#messages}

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| **No events yet** | Record your first events. **Go to the ledger** opens the **Ledger**, **Resume the set-up** reopens the first-launch questions. See [First steps](./first-steps.mdx). |
| **History is being rebuilt** | Wait. The value appears once every line has a price. See [Rebuild and resolution](./rebuild-and-resolution.mdx). |
| **No price:** followed by symbols | These lines are counted at their purchase price. Check their symbol in the **Ledger**. |
| **Calculation in progress — provisional figures.** | The price history is being rebuilt. The figures update on their own. |
| **The last recompute failed** | The figures are those of the previous computation. The [System](./system.mdx) page says what failed. |
