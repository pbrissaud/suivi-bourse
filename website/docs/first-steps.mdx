title: First steps

The first time you open SuiviBourse, it asks four questions in a row. This page
walks you through them, then shows what happens once your first events are in.

Only the first question is required. You can skip the other three and come back
to them later.

## Choose your base currency {#currency}

![Step 1 of 4, asking which currency you keep your accounts in](/img/app/en/first-run-currency.png)

SuiviBourse reports every figure in one currency, the base currency. Prices
quoted in another currency are converted into it.

1. In **Currency**, pick the currency your accounts are kept in. The field
   suggests one from your browser's language.
2. Click **Continue**.

Once saved, the base currency cannot be changed in the app. Every amount you
record is read in that currency, so pick it with care.

The app shows only this screen until you answer.

## Pick your taxation models {#taxation}

![Step 2 of 4, listing the taxation models published for one country](/img/app/en/first-run-taxation.png)

A taxation model tells SuiviBourse how a gain is taxed when you sell, so it can
show what you would keep after tax.

1. In **Country**, pick your country. The list shows the models published for it.
2. Tick the models that match your accounts. Each line shows its rate.
3. Click **Continue**.

To go on without a model, click **Continue** with nothing ticked. To write your
own, click **Write my own model**: it closes the walk and opens **Settings**.
See [Your taxation model](./your-taxation-model.mdx).

## Create your first account {#account}

![Step 3 of 4, with the Identifier, Label and Taxation model fields](/img/app/en/first-run-accounts.png)

An account is where your events are recorded, for example a brokerage account
or a tax-sheltered savings plan.

1. In **Identifier**, type a short id such as `broker-a`. Use letters, digits,
   `-`, `_` and `.`, with no space. You cannot change it afterwards.
2. In **Label**, type the name to show on screen (optional).
3. In **Taxation model**, pick one of the models you ticked, or **No model**.
4. Click **Continue**.

To go on without an account, click **Skip this step**. Your events then go into
the **Unassigned** account, and you can move them later. See
[Accounts](./accounts.mdx).

## Add your first events {#events}

![Step 4 of 4, offering to import a statement, enter by hand or do it later](/img/app/en/first-run-events.png)

Choose where your first events come from:

| Choice | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **Import a statement** | The import page, for a `.csv` or Excel file. See [Import your events](./import-your-events.mdx). |
| **Enter by hand** | The event form on the **Ledger**, for a deposit or a purchase. See [Ledger](./ledger.mdx). |
| **Later** | The **Dashboard**. You can add events from the **Ledger** at any time. |

Start with a **Cash in** event for the money you paid into the account, then
your purchases. Without deposits, the app cannot compute your net contributed
amount or your returns.

If you leave the walk early, the empty **Dashboard** offers **Resume the set-up**.

## What happens next {#next}

Once you have recorded events:

1. SuiviBourse fetches the current price of each security you hold.
2. It rebuilds the price history back to your first purchase. This runs in the
   background. Until it ends, the **Dashboard** says how many series are
   complete. See
   [Rebuild and resolution](./rebuild-and-resolution.mdx).
3. The **Dashboard** shows your portfolio value, its chart and your gain.

A line with no price yet is counted at its purchase price. If it stays without
a price, check its symbol in the **Ledger**.

## Find your way around {#navigate}

The sidebar leads to **Dashboard**, **Shares**, **Accounts**, **Ledger**,
**Comparison**, **Agent**, **Settings** and **System**.

![The search palette listing securities, accounts, pages and actions](/img/app/en/palette.png)

Press ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux) to open **Search**. Type the name of a security, an account, an event or a page, or
pick an action such as **Record an event** or **Add an account**.

To change the language or the theme, go to **Settings → Display**. See
[Settings](./settings.mdx).
