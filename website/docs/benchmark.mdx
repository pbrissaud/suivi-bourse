title: Comparison
description: Compare your securities with an index fund bought with the same money on the same days

The **Comparison** page tells you, in euros, whether an index fund would have
left you with more. It replays your purchases, sales and dividends into the fund,
on the same days and for the same amounts.

![The Comparison page: the gap against the S&P 500, the key figures and the gap chart](/img/app/en/benchmark.png)

## Follow a comparison fund {#follow}

The page compares you with funds you follow. You choose them in **Settings**.

1. Open **Settings** and go to **Portfolio → Comparison funds**.
2. Click **Add a fund**. The **Comparison funds** sheet opens.
3. Turn on the switch next to each fund you want. Each row says since which
   year the fund is quoted.

| Fund | Symbol | Quoted since |
|---|---|---|
| MSCI World | `CW8.PA` | 2009 |
| S&P 500 | `CSPX.AS` | 2010 |
| Euro Stoxx 50 | `MSE.PA` | 2008 |
| CAC 40 | `C40.PA` | 2008 |
| MSCI Emerging Markets | `AEEM.PA` | 2010 |

The list is fixed: you cannot add another ticker.

The fund's price history then downloads in the background, one fund at a time.
Its row in **Comparison funds** shows the progress (**downloading 40 %**), then
**full history**. The Comparison page offers a fund only once its history is
complete. The download waits until your ledger holds at least one event.

## Stop following a fund {#unfollow}

1. In **Settings → Portfolio → Comparison funds**, click **Stop following** on
   the fund's row.
2. Confirm.

The fund leaves the list of the Comparison page, and its downloaded history is
kept. To delete that history, click **Add a fund**, then **Stop keeping the
history** under the fund. It goes at the next cleanup, shown on the **System**
page. **Undo** keeps it.

## Read the comparison {#read}

The first line, **Your securities against the …**, names the fund. Click the fund
name to switch to another fund you follow. **Add a fund** at the bottom of that
list opens **Settings**.

Under the heading, the page gives the period: **Compared from … to …**.

The main sentence gives the gap: **1 250 € more than putting the same money in
the MSCI World**, or **less**. Under it, the gap as a share of the money
invested.

| Figure | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Same money invested** | Your purchases and free shares at their declared value, minus your sales and dividends, each on its own day. A deposit left in cash is not part of it. |
| **Gain on your securities** | What your securities gained over the period, as a percentage of the same money invested |
| **Gain on the …** | What the fund would have gained with the same flows, as a percentage of the same money invested |

The two bars, **Your securities** and **In the …**, show both values side by
side.

The gains cover the period and are not annual rates. The comparison leaves
your cash out.
[Comparison with a fund](./read-your-figures.mdx#benchmark) explains the
computation with an example.

## Read the chart {#chart}

The chart has two views:

- **Gap**: the gap in euros, day by day. Above zero, your securities are ahead.
- **Amounts**: two curves, **Your portfolio** and the fund, for example
  **MSCI World, same money invested**.

Pick the period with **1 month**, **This year**, **1 year** or **All**.

## Account by account {#accounts}

The **Account by account** table repeats the comparison for each account, over
the account's own period. The **Together** row adds them up to the figure at the
top of the page.

An account that held no securities while the fund was quoted is not in the
table.

## Why the period is shorter {#period}

The page says when the period does not cover your whole ledger:

| Message | Cause |
|---|---|
| **Compared from …, this fund's first close** | Your ledger starts before the fund was quoted. The message says how many of your years are left out. Pick an older fund to cover them. |
| **The period ends on …: money leaving your securities that day is more than the fund could have funded** | A sale or dividend was larger than the fund holding at that point. |
| **The period ends on …: the next day's close has no exchange rate** | The fund's price for the next day cannot be converted into your base currency yet. |

## When there is no figure {#empty}

| What the page shows | What to do |
|---|---|
| **No comparison fund followed** | Click **Choose the funds** and follow one. |
| **… is downloading** | Wait for the download to finish. **Manage the funds** opens **Settings**. |
| **Nothing to compare yet** | Record your first events. |
| **Being computed** | Wait: the daily values of your accounts are not computed yet. |
| **Nothing to compare** | None of your accounts held securities since the fund's first quote. |
| **No price received** | The market sent no price for this fund. Check the **System** page, or follow another fund. |
| **Total not comparable** | An exchange rate is missing on the first day. |
