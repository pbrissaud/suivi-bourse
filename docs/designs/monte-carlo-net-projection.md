## Problem Statement

#757 asks for "a Monte Carlo net-worth forecast (5 / 10 / 20 years)", a
p10 / median / p90 cone, "fed by the detected DCA".

Three gaps between what the ticket asks for and what the app can hold.

**The cone would be gross.** A pre-tax projection is an amount nobody will ever
receive. In a product whose whole discipline is to refuse a figure it cannot
stand behind, that is the defect that matters.

**"Net worth" promises more than the ledger holds.** The event types are `BUY`,
`SELL`, `GRANT`, `DIVIDEND`, `DEPOSIT`, `WITHDRAWAL`. No savings account, no
property, no debt.

**The "detected DCA" does not measure what the ticket thinks.** ADR-0041 settles
that the rhythm is measured **on the buys**: `rhythm._monthly_amounts` counts
`EventType.BUY` alone, and the record says buys are *"an internal flow: money
already inside, changing form"*, where deposits are the money crossing the
boundary. Feeding `monthly_amount` in as an **external contribution** over 240
months therefore counts rebalancing as fresh savings — the ADR already names the
limit: *"a month of rebalancing reports more rhythm than the owner lived"*.

Two aggravations of the same measure: the median covers **at most twelve
months** (`WINDOW_MONTHS = 12`) and **only the covered ones**, so an irregular
investor's contribution is overstated and then extrapolated over twenty years;
and `monthly_amount` is `Optional` — `None` on a fresh ledger.

So the projected contribution is not `rhythm`. That is Q1, and the way out is
short: a gross running sum of `DEPOSIT` per account is a one-line query, on the
exact model of `ledger.first_payments()`.

## What Makes This Cool

**The kink.** `aged_flat_realised` makes the rate depend on the wrapper's age,
so a projection at 5, 10 and 20 years crosses the account's own threshold.

But **only income tax disappears, not the social charges.** `taxation.py`
declares `social_rate` as the fifth parameter of `aged_flat_realised` for
exactly that reason, and ADR-0042 names the trap: a single-rate model would
report a mature PEA as untaxed, wrong by the whole of the social charges. The
kink is `rate_before + social_rate → rate_after + social_rate`, and `social_rate`
being optional, its absence reads as zero.

**Which date dates the kink — an open question, not a settled fact.** The
`fr_pea` template sets `age_basis: FIRST_PAYMENT`, and `taxation.py` warns this
is not the opening date. Two dates already exist in the code and they disagree:

- `accounts.opening_dates_by_account()` reads `account_fact.opened_on`,
  **declared and editable** (#918), pre-filled but contradictable — #918 exists
  precisely because a PEA opened in 2015 and transferred, whose ledger only
  starts in 2022, would derive three years of age instead of ten;
- `ledger.first_payments()` derives `min(date)` over `DEPOSIT`, and today serves
  two readers: pre-filling the form, and feeding
  `advisories.OPENED_AFTER_FIRST_PAYMENT`.

Deriving it again would silently overwrite the owner's declaration and
reintroduce the bug #918 closed. That is **Q9**, not a next step.

Either way the date arrives as an **argument**: `projection.py` is pure and both
functions above take a store.

**The wall.** The PEA is capped at €150 000 of payments.

Same vigilance: the ceiling counts **payments** (#921: *"a gross running sum of
payments"*), the rhythm counts **buys**. A `BUY` inside a PEA consumes no
ceiling. Caveat to own: **#921 is `needs-triage`, no milestone**, and its body
says none of it is settled. Making it a premise imports an untriaged ticket.

Nobody joins the two halves of this problem: the trackers
([Wealthfolio](https://wealthfolio.app/) already ships a Monte Carlo) have the
ledger but not the wrappers; the French PEA simulators compute the tax correctly
but on a blank form. The join only became possible with #752 and #918.

## Constraints

- **ADR-0042 — no rate ships**, and the record **sources the figures itself**:
  *"12,8 % of income tax plus 18,6 % of social charges, where the wrapper changes
  only the second — assurance-vie stays at 17,2 % (LFSS 2026, art. 12)"*. Two
  consequences here: **(a)** the social rate is **not universal** —
  `fr_assurance_vie` is a shipped template and its 17,2 % carve-out is a
  sub-case of the very trap `social_rate` exists for; a design that congratulates
  itself on catching one must handle the other. **(b)** the rate moved on
  1 January 2026, which is the ADR's whole argument: none of it is bundled, all
  of it is declared.
- **A pure module** in the sense `CLAUDE.md` gives the word: no store, no
  yfinance, `now` injected — and **`rng` injected**, for the same reason.
- **`pandas` is a heavy edge.** `conventions.sh` asserts that the pure modules
  pull neither `duckdb`, nor `yfinance`, nor `pandas`, nor `openpyxl`. The engine
  is therefore **numpy only**; `numpy` is not on that list.
- **`numpy` needs a direct line in `pyproject.toml`.** Today it is transitive
  only, and the `pyarrow` comment sets the convention: a line exists *"only to
  stop a direct dependency from being satisfied by accident"*. In place: numpy
  2.5.0, pandas 3.0.3.
- **`conventions.sh` will not check `projection.py` on its own**: the pure-module
  list is hardcoded there. `"projection"` has to be added to it.
- **One process** (ADR-0039), and the two paths do not carry the same risk.
  `boot.py` mounts Flask behind `WSGIMiddleware(app, workers=WSGI_THREADS)` with
  `WSGI_THREADS = 4`: an `/api` route occupies one of four pool threads, and
  numpy releases the GIL. By contrast `self._mcp_app(scope, …)` is **awaited
  directly on the loop** — a synchronous MCP handler does block it. That is
  exactly the path Next Step 6 plans to expose. On memory: 19 MB per account at
  `n = 10 000` over 240 months in float64, but the sum is **streamable** (one
  `(n, months)` accumulator is enough, so two matrices whatever the account
  count) provided the aggregation signature does not force materialisation.
- **Currency is not a problem** (ADR-0002): events record *the debit, in the base
  currency*, and only prices are converted, at write time. Summing accounts
  requires no conversion — that is precisely what the ADR buys.
- **ADR-0024**: every outgoing label is a message key, in both catalogues.

## Premises

1. **The projection is net, or it is worthless.**
2. **This design describes at least two tickets**: #919, then #757.
3. **"Net worth" is a promise the app cannot keep.**
4. **No rate ships here either.** An account with no declared model gets a
   **named absence**, not a cone degraded to gross.
5. **The slider has a wall** — measured on payments, not on buys. Subject to
   #921, untriaged.
6. **The engine projects per account, and it is the *trajectories* that sum.**
   *Revised during the session.* The sum of quantiles is not the quantile of the
   sum: adding per-account p10s assumes ρ = 1 across accounts and erases the
   diversification. Sum trajectory by trajectory, quantile only at the end.
7. **A net projection assumes liquidation.** The **three** `*_realised` kinds —
   `flat_realised`, `aged_flat_realised`, `bracketed_realised` — tax a
   **realised** gain: a net cone at t assumes a full disposal on that date, and
   the screen has to say so. The other two kinds of the enumeration,
   `withholding_income` and `none`, produce no kink at all.
8. **The cost basis grows with the contributions.** At t it is
   `V_t − (base_0 + Σ contributions)`, not `V_t − base_0`: without that the cone
   over-taxes by the whole of the projected contribution. `position.cost_basis`
   exists and `portfolio_view._ADDITIVE` aggregates it, so `base_0` is a given;
   `base_t` is what has to be built.
9. **Tax has a floor at zero** (#919: *zero on a loss, never a negative*).
   Consequence to own: in loss territory, `p10_net == p10_gross`. The
   differentiator shows on the median and the p90, barely on the p10.

## Cross-Model Perspective

Two cold reads by subagents with no access to the conversation (same model
family; their disagreement is worth more than their agreement). What survived
and changed the design:

- **Premise 6 was challenged in round one, rightly** — hence its revision. The
  error runs pessimistic, which is what makes it insidious: it looks prudent.
- **Without common random numbers, simulation noise can exceed the delta of one
  slider notch.** Two compared positions share the draw.
- **Bundle no return assumption either**: *"ADR-0042 refuses to bundle 31,4 %
  because a budget law moves it, and the same feature would bundle μ = 7 %,
  σ = 15 %, a far more arbitrary figure, undated and unsourced."* That is what
  makes `pool` an argument (see the proposed ADR).
- **A block bootstrap over synchronous windows supplies the cross-account
  correlation** premise 6 needs, for free — a material argument for approach B,
  and the answer to Q3.

The rest of their material (named trajectories, the bootstrap's shape) belongs to
approach B and is not carried here.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A — The pure primitive *(chosen)*

`projection.py` and `taxation_projection.py`, pure, plus the per-account tax
projection #919 asks for.

### Approach B — The full net cone, bootstrapped

A, plus the return sample drawn from real market history through `market.py`
(synchronous blocks), named trajectories, the kink, the wall, the screen and its
slider. **Set aside for now**: too long against the other v5 fronts, and silent
without a declared model. Still the target.

### Approach C — The calendar, not the cone

The certain dates as the product (`crossings()`), the cone as background later.
**Set aside for now**, but it is the only one of the three that needs **no rate
and no return assumption**, hence the only one immune to "nothing to show for
someone who declared nothing". The natural follow-on to A.

## Recommended Approach

**A** — and **#757 is deferred**, explicitly, until Q1 and Q2 are settled and
either B or C is launched. A delivers no cone, no screen, no visible figure: it
delivers the core of #919 and a primitive.

**Two modules, not one.** #919's tax projection (`rate`, `social_rate`,
threshold, floor at zero) is **scalar** arithmetic: it needs no numpy and must
not drag that dependency into a 5.1 ticket.

```python
# src/application/taxation_projection.py — pure, scalar arithmetic, NO numpy.
def projected_tax(*, value, cost_basis, model, age_years) -> float:
    """One account's tax at one instant. Floor at zero (#919).
    `social_rate` adds on BOTH sides of the threshold."""

# src/application/projection.py — pure; numpy only; now and rng injected.
def draw(*, months, n, pool, rng) -> np.ndarray:
    """The block indices, drawn ONCE and shared by every account: that is what
    supplies the cross-account correlation (premise 6, Q3). One rng per account
    would give rho = 0, the mirror image of the error being corrected."""

def paths(*, opening_value, monthly, draws, pool) -> np.ndarray:
    """(n, months) for ONE account, under the SHARED draws.
    `monthly` is a vector of length `months`, not a scalar: that is what makes
    Q6 — the contribution after the wall — expressible without changing the API."""

def accumulate(acc, account_paths, tax) -> np.ndarray:
    """Applies the tax PER ACCOUNT (floor at zero account by account), THEN adds
    to the accumulator. Streamable: never more than two matrices in memory,
    whatever the account count."""

def quantiles(acc, qs) -> dict:
    """The last gesture, and the only place a quantile is ever taken."""
```

**Where the tax applies: per account, between `paths()` and the sum.** Premise 9
imposes a floor at zero account by account; applying it after the sum would let
a loss on one account offset a gain on another, which #919 refuses. That is
`accumulate()`'s reason to exist.

**The recurrence, written correctly.** A trajectory with a periodic contribution
is `V_{t+1} = V_t · (1 + r_t) + m_t` — this is **not** a `cumsum`. Vectorised:
`C_t = cumprod(1 + r)` over `t = 1..months`, then
`V_t = C_t · (V_0 + Σ_{k=1..t} m_k / C_k)`. **Convention to freeze: the
contribution lands at end of period**, so `m_1` is paid after the first return.
Without that sentence two implementers write two different things, and the gap is
one month of contribution over 240.

Keep the test against the naive loop — not for a numerical risk (in float64 over
240 monthly steps `C_k` stays in a benign range) but because it is cheap and it
locks the indexing down.

**`pool` stays an argument, never a constant.** That is a structural decision:
**write an ADR**. The repo holds 41 and this one is of the same order.

## Open Questions

1. **Which contribution to project?** `rhythm.monthly_amount` measures buys, an
   internal flow (ADR-0041), over twelve months at most, empty months excluded,
   `None` on a fresh ledger. The serious candidate is a gross running sum of
   `DEPOSIT` per account — a one-line query, on the model of `first_payments()`.
   **Hidden assumption to write down if that path is taken:** growing `base_t` by
   the deposits (premise 8) assumes every deposit is invested immediately. The
   app has **no notion of a cash balance**. A deposit sitting idle creates no
   cost basis.
2. **Where does the return sample come from?** Declared, or bootstrapped through
   `market.py`. **Blocking**: Q3 depends on it, and so does the MCP tool.
3. **Where does the cross-account correlation come from?** If each account
   consumes the same `rng` sequentially, the accounts are **independent**
   (ρ = 0) — the mirror image of the error premise 6 corrects, inflating the p10
   this time. A synchronous block bootstrap solves it for free; a parametric draw
   needs a correlation matrix nothing supplies today.
4. **What `n`?** Large enough that simulation noise stays under the delta of one
   slider notch. To be measured, not assumed. *Memory is no longer the
   constraint*: with `accumulate()`, two matrices suffice whatever the account
   count. CPU probably isn't either (2.4 M flops at `n = 10 000`) — but a
   synchronous MCP handler is awaited on the loop, so measure it there, not on
   `/api`.
5. **How is `base_t` built?** The cost basis grows with the contributions
   (premise 8); `base_0` comes from `position.cost_basis`, the rest is to derive.
6. **What does the contribution do after the wall?** Stop, move to another
   account, or have the screen refuse that slider position? The `monthly` vector
   makes all three expressible; one has to be chosen.
7. **What does the screen show for an account with no declared model?** Premise 4
   says "named absence"; whether the feature disappears or invites a declaration
   is undecided. This is the main risk of A and B.
8. **#757's title says "net worth".** To be corrected, or paired with a statement
   of what is excluded.
9. **Which date dates the kink?** `account_fact.opened_on`, declared and editable
   (#918), or `ledger.first_payments()`, derived? #919 says "its opening date";
   `taxation.py` says a PEA runs from the first payment; #918 exists because
   deriving overwrites a legitimate declaration. The three cannot all be right.
10. **Is the contribution invested immediately?** See Q1 — with no cash balance,
    that is an assumption, not a fact.

*Settled while writing:* the seed derives from **the slider position alone**,
never from the account. A per-account seed would give independent draws, hence
ρ = 0 across accounts — the mirror image of the error premise 6 corrects. Draws
happen once, in `draw()`, and are shared. Currency is not a problem (ADR-0002:
events record the debit in the base currency).

## Success Criteria

- `src/application/projection.py` exists, pure, **and `"projection"` has been
  added to the hardcoded list in `conventions.sh`** — otherwise nothing checks it.
- A direct `numpy` line in `pyproject.toml`, with its justifying comment, per the
  convention `pyarrow` sets.
- **The test ADR-0042 asks for by name: a mature PEA still pays `social_rate`** —
  and a second one for the assurance-vie carve-out. This is the bug the document
  congratulates itself on catching; it has to be locked by a test, not a sentence.
- A test on the growing basis: at a constant contribution over N months, the
  projected tax does not tax the contribution.
- The premise 6 test on `accumulate()`, **explicitly parameterised**: ρ = 0.5,
  fixed `n`, frozen seed, and a tolerance derived from the measured noise — not a
  strict inequality on a noisy estimate.
- A reproducibility test: same seed, same output; two slider positions under the
  same seed give a delta that is stable across runs.
- A test of the vectorised recurrence against the naive loop, over 240 steps.
- A measurement of memory and compute time at the chosen `n`, in the real process,
  on the MCP path.
- `uv run flake8 src/application src/api --ignore=E501` and `uv run pytest tests/`
  green.

*Dropped as a criterion: "no rate hardcoded in `src/`".* The `undefined` sentinel
rule works because it looks for a word; a float that happens to be a rate has no
lexical form, so the rule would not be checkable. The real control is the
signature — `pool` and the model's parameters arriving as arguments.

## Design decisions — #919's screen

Added 2026-09-14 by `/plan-design-review`. The eng review settled the arithmetic
and left the screen unmade: four of #919's ten acceptance criteria are the panel
and the two catalogues, and the nine decisions named no placement, no altitude,
no message key, no colour and no sentence. Six decisions and one amendment close
that, in the same shape as the nine above.

**D10 — The projection is a card of its own, full width, between the three-card
grid and the lines card.** Not a fourth member of the grid: four cards in
`lg:grid-cols-3` leave an orphan on a second row and break the one-shape read
that grid exists for (#838), and 34 px would put a hypothetical disposal at the
altitude of three measured facts. Not a line in the head either: the head is the
account's identity and its four lines are true of every account, while this one
is present on some and absent on others. The card takes the ordinary
`Card` / `CardHeader` / `eyebrow` / `CardContent` shape, its figure at
`tabular text-4xl font-heavy tracking-tight` — the rung already reserved on this
panel for the one subordinate headline, `Total performance`. **A projection never
outranks a measurement.**

**D11 — Every account carrying a model gets the card. Only an account carrying
none gets total silence.** #919's AC 4 and AC 5 say *no model*; they do not say
*no figure*. Sending `none`, `withholding_income` and a dateless
`aged_flat_realised` to the same blank as *never answered* makes a declaration
indistinguishable from its absence, and contradicts the ticket's own rule one
line up: an account in aggregate **loss** shows `0 €`, where zero is *less*
certain than under a `none` model that shows nothing. The branch table:

| Model | Card | Figure |
|---|---|---|
| none declared | absent | — |
| `kind: none` | present | `0 €`, caveat replaced by what that model says |
| `withholding_income` | present | no realised-gain figure; the card names what the model taxes |
| the three `*_realised` | present | the projected tax, floor at zero |

**D12 — The card waits on the positions read, not on its own.** `projected_tax`
rides on `/api/accounts` and therefore lands **before** the figures it is derived
from: the head gates even `row.total_value` behind `terms !== null`
(`AccountDetail.tsx:308`) precisely so a head cannot contradict itself. A tax
rendered beside a `Gain` that is still blank states a figure derived from a gain
it is simultaneously refusing to state. Same gate, and the refused read wears
`<Unreadable failure={failures.positions} />` in the card's own place (ADR-0037),
not a second copy of the head's.

**D13 — The caveat is visible body text, never an `Explain` bubble.** `Explain`
opens on click and nothing else, and every bubble in this product explains a
*convention behind a number the reader can otherwise trust*. Here, not clicking
costs the entire epistemic status of the figure. `Projected tax 4 168,95 €` reads
as a fact. The panel already has the right precedent twice, both plain
`text-xs text-muted-foreground`: `accounts.detail.payers.period` and
`accounts.detail.curve.period`. This is an **extent**, not a rule. It also avoids
a thirteenth `DocsAnchor`, a new heading on `read-your-figures.mdx`, and a French
reader sent to English prose about their own tax.

**The sentence, drafted, because #919 calls it load-bearing and the document had
deleted it.** Premise 7 — *"a net cone at t assumes a full disposal on that date,
and the screen has to say so"* — belonged to Approach A and died with it. It is
the one clause that matters:

> **en** — What you would owe if you sold everything in this account today, at the
> rates you recorded. Nothing has been sold, and this is not a tax return.
>
> **fr** — Ce que vous devriez si vous cédiez tout ce compte aujourd'hui, aux taux
> que vous avez déclarés. Rien n'a été cédé, et ceci n'est pas une déclaration.

**D14 — The card's footing carries the applied rate, and for
`aged_flat_realised` the day the rate changes.** One row on the
`mt-auto … border-t pt-3` shape the two cards beside it already use. It costs
nothing — the parameters are on the account — and it answers three omissions at
once: ADR-0042 ships no rate, so the reader learns *which* rate produced the
figure; the kink is the most interesting thing the arithmetic knows and is
otherwise invisible; and an owner three months from their PEA's fifth
anniversary sees one number with no hint it is about to fall.

**D15 — The absence is typed, not collapsed.** `projected_tax?: number` on
`Account`, and `buildAccountRows` must **not** map it `?? null` like its twelve
neighbours (`lib/accounts.ts:346-360`): that idiom collapses *absent* into
*null*, which is the exact distinction decision 6 exists to preserve on the
server. `inFlightShape.test.ts` reads the declared slot off the real program, so
a `?? null` here passes `tsc` and erases D6 in silence.

**Amendment to D2 — and it reverses a decision settled by the eng review tonight.**
D2/9A has the arithmetic read `account_fact.opened_on` **alone**, with `age_basis`
read by the advisory rather than by the arithmetic. Verified on the source, that
makes **every PEA declared through the shipped template render nothing, for ever,
with no repair reachable from the interface**:

- `taxation.py:99-100` — `fr_pea` sets `age_basis: FIRST_PAYMENT`;
- `AccountForm.tsx:153` — `ageMatters = age_basis === 'opening'`;
- `AccountForm.tsx:343` — the `openedOn` field renders only when `ageMatters`;
- `AccountForm.tsx:245-246` — `opened_on` is sent only when `ageMatters`;
- so `opened_on` stays null, D5 finds no date, and the card never appears.

`AccountForm.tsx:199` already pre-fills the draft with
`account.opened_on ?? account.first_payment` and then discards it. The PEA is this
feature's flagship case and the only shipped template that falls.

**The fix is the arithmetic, not the form**: the age date is `opened_on` where
`age_basis === 'opening'`, and `opened_on ?? first_payment` where it is
`first_payment` — which is what `age_basis` exists to say. This does **not**
reintroduce #918's bug: #918 is a *declared* date overwritten by a derived one,
and here there is no declaration to overwrite. Amending the form instead would
contradict #918's own reasoning and is the worse trade.

**One thing #919's brief promises that nothing builds.** The ticket justifies its
silence with *"the advisory raised elsewhere is what says why, once, in the place
built for saying it"*. `advisories.py` declares exactly two codes — `CASH_SHARE`
(:21) and `OPENED_AFTER_FIRST_PAYMENT` (:23) — and no open ticket adds a third.
Either a `no_taxation_model` advisory under `SUBJECT_ACCOUNTS` ships with #919
(~15 lines beside `_observe_cash_share`; the rail already renders account
advisories as chips) or the sentence comes out of the brief. Shipping the claim
with nothing behind it is the one option that is not available.

**TWO DECISIONS TAKEN AT IMPLEMENTATION (2026-09-15), AND THEY CLOSE THE TWO
THINGS D14 AND THE BRIEF LEFT OPEN.**

**D16 — a ladder's footing names its rungs, not a rate.** D14 asks the footing
for *the rate that produced the figure*, and for `flat_realised` and
`aged_flat_realised` that rate is readable off the parameters. For
`bracketed_realised` it is not: which rung produced the last euro depends on the
assiette, which only the server holds. The footing therefore names **every rung
of the ladder** — `10 % · 30 % · 42 %` — which is read off the parameters alone,
adds no member to the wire the nine decisions fixed, and asks the reader to
place their own gain among rates that are all true of the model. The two
alternatives were rejected: a `projected_rate` member widens the contract for
one kind, and dropping the footing on ladders leaves the reader of a progressive
regime — the one regime where the rate is least obvious — with no rate at all.

**The advisory ships, and the brief keeps its sentence.**

model**, beside `_observe_cash_share` and on the same `SUBJECT_ACCOUNTS`. An
account with no value raises nothing: it is not missing a declaration, it is
empty, and the seeded `default` row of a fresh install is exactly that case. The
bell carries the two sentences; the rail's chip stays `cash_share`-specific,
that chip being a percentage and not a generic advisory renderer.

**WHAT `/review` CHANGED (2026-09-15), AND TWO OF THE FOUR WERE BUGS.**

**D17 — the rates are published, not re-derived in the browser.** D14's footing
was first built on four TypeScript helpers in `lib/accounts.ts` that
re-implemented `taxation_projection`'s age rule: which side of the threshold,
the levy folded in, the 29 February fallback. That is the same rule read twice
in two languages, and it drifts on exactly the accounts nobody tests — the card
would name a rate the figure beside it contradicts, and **neither would look
wrong**. `/api/accounts` now carries `taxation_kind`, `projected_rates` and
`projected_rate_changes_on` beside `projected_tax`, through the same `_declared`
fold. Ninety lines of TypeScript and the panel's `taxation-models` request are
gone with them, and the card renders as soon as the accounts payload lands
rather than waiting on a second read.

**Two refusals `taxation.validate` was missing, both reachable, both probed.**
The ladder's climbing check compares each rung against the one before, so index
0 had nothing to be refused by: a first ceiling of `-100` was accepted and made
the slice under it negative, so `projected_tax` returned **−100 € on a 1 000 €
gain** — the floor at zero contravened by a value the record let through.
`_brackets` now refuses a first ceiling at or below zero, and `_bracketed`
floors its own result regardless, the module's stated rule not resting on a
validator one import away. Separately `threshold_years` had no ceiling:
`1e15` was accepted, and the front's `Date.UTC()` then returned `NaN`, whose
`.toISOString()` throws `RangeError` inside a render body with no ErrorBoundary
above it — the whole page, not the card. `MAX_THRESHOLD_YEARS = 200`.

**The positions read is conditional.** `/api/accounts` is asked by the
dashboard, the accounts page, the settings, the ledger, the ⌘K palette and the
first-run walk. Reading the portfolio's hot query there to derive an assiette
only `flat_realised` and `bracketed_realised` consume made every one of those
readers pay for a feature they may not have declared. `_latent_gains` resolves
the models first and returns `{}` when no declared account needs the read.

**An account holding nothing owes a known zero.** Deposits and no purchase is
what a freshly declared wrapper looks like, and it has no line in
`build_shares` — so the assiette was *absent* and the card said *I cannot tell*
about a sum the app can do in its head. `latent_gains_by_account` seeds every
declared account at zero; one unvalued line still makes the whole account
unknown, which is the seed's opposite half and unchanged.

**Left undone, and named rather than fixed.** `mcp_server.py:304-332`
re-assembles `/api/accounts` by hand and calls no part of `_with_account_facts`,
so the MCP surface already misses `taxation_model`, `opened_on` and
`first_payment` and now misses four more. That divergence predates #919 and
#920 is the ticket that owns the route.

**WHAT THE ADVERSARIAL PASS CHANGED (2026-09-15), AND ONE OF THEM REVERSES
DECISION 3.**

**D18 — one assiette, three kinds, and `gain_absolu` is not it.** Decision 3
gave the aged family `gain_absolu` and believed it was *rejecting*
`total_value − net_contributed`. They are the same arithmetic:
`performance.py:306` writes `gain_absolu = total_value - contributed`, and
`:228` says `contributed` is net of **withdrawals**. So a gain realised and
taken out of a PEA years ago stayed in its assiette for ever. On an emptied
account — deposit 10 000, sell at 15 000, withdraw 15 000 — the card billed
~860 € of tax on a wrapper holding nothing, under a caption reading *"if you
sold everything in this account today"*, while the same account under a
`flat_realised` model answered `0 €`. All three kinds now read
`Σ market_value − Σ cost_basis`. The kind decides the **rate** and nothing else.

**Four more the adversarial pass found, all probed before being believed:**

- `threshold_day` ran off the calendar. `MAX_THRESHOLD_YEARS` bounds the *span*
  and nothing bounds the *start*: a wrapper declared as opened in 9999 plus a
  two-century threshold is `start.replace(year=10199)`, an uncaught `ValueError`
  that **500s `/api/accounts`** — the read every page makes, including the one
  its owner would repair the row from. The guard is now on the read, because the
  write is checked once and a row predating the bound is not.
- A closed line on a delisted symbol erased the whole account's assiette.
  `positions()` keeps the row after a full exit and the aggregator zeroes its
  basis, so the holding is worth exactly nothing and that is **known** — but it
  has no price, and the strict rule read *unknown* off it. A holding of nothing
  is now skipped; a holding nobody priced still makes the account unknown.
- `rate` and `social_rate` were each bounded to `[0, 1]` and their sum was not:
  `0,9` beside `0,9` projected **180 %** of a gain and printed it as a fact.
- `_newest_account_metrics`'s docstring claimed the scan happens once per
  listing. It is one *spelling*, not one scan — each observer still calls it,
  and the docstring now says so rather than being a saving a future reader
  trusts.

**Named and not fixed.** The threshold flips on the **UTC** day, so an owner
east of Greenwich can see the old rate for up to a day past their anniversary.
A `taxation_model` id pointing at a deleted model row silences both the card and
the `no_taxation_model` advisory, so the hole reports itself as nothing at all.
A bracket ceiling is a bare number with no currency attached, and the base
currency is a mutable setting. Three tracker items, none of them #919's.
