## 1. Where it lives

A **sixth route**, `/benchmark`, whose sidebar entry is **`Référence`** (source
string `Benchmark`), in the `PORTFOLIO` group — *what the owner looks at* — in
fourth position, after `Comptes`.

Two consequences that must land in the same diff:

- `AppSidebar.tsx:76` asserts *"The five entries are five, at every N."* A sixth
  entry makes that sentence false. Rewrite it; do not leave it standing.
- `DashboardPage.tsx:328-331` argues against a sixth page on the grounds that it
  *"would open a page holding one block"*. This page holds a head, a chart, a
  control row and #983's slot. The argument does not reach it — but say so,
  because the next reader will check.

**The label is a noun**, like `Titres`, `Comptes`, `Grand livre`. `Comparaison`
names an operation, which no other entry does, and it under-describes the page
the day #983 lands.

```
  │           versements"                                       │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  ┌─ #983's slot, empty until it lands ─────────────────────────┐
```

**The head figure is in euros.** `Stat.tsx:17-20` states the rule this obeys:
*"the year-to-date euro and the year-to-date percentage are two figures that
must never share a line."* The percentage gap is filed one weight down, in a
`Stat size="term"`, with the two sides' own returns. Three reasons the euro
leads and not the percentage: the product's one head figure is already
`Gain total` in euros (`Head.tsx`); the gross/net toggle answers *how much is
left*, which is a euro question, and led in percent it would visibly move a
figure it does not govern; and a percentage gap is ambiguous on its face
(difference of two TWRs? in points? annualised?).

**The head is one `Card`, not a row of three.** `Head.tsx:223` already refused
the three-card row once. Subordination is vertical (`Stat.tsx`): a total and its
terms never share a line.

**The controls come last in reading order and sit on a row of their own**
between the head and the chart — the idiom of `DashboardPage.tsx:268-292`. The
page answers before it asks.

## 3. The verdict sentence

The only sentence in this product that tells its owner they were wrong. It is
written here, not left to whoever implements it. Three cases, because
`sign.ts:38` says a zero is a figure and not an absence.

| Case | French (fr.json) | English source |
|---|---|---|
| ahead | `Vos titres ont fait {gap} de plus que le {index}, sur la même période et avec les mêmes versements.` | `Your holdings made {gap} more than the {index}, over the same period and with the same contributions.` |
| behind | `Vos titres ont fait {gap} de moins que le {index}, sur la même période et avec les mêmes versements.` | `Your holdings made {gap} less than the {index}, over the same period and with the same contributions.` |
| equal | `Vos titres et le {index} finissent au même montant, à l'euro près.` | `Your holdings and the {index} end at the same amount, to the euro.` |

**The trailing clause is load-bearing, not decoration.** Without *avec les mêmes
versements* the reader reads the gap as a return gap, which it is not — it is a
replay of their own flows. Drop that clause and the screen lies without a single
line of wrong code.

The tie threshold is **to the euro**, as the sentence says.

## 4. The reference selector

`ui/select.tsx` is **vendored** — one new primitive, named here so it is not
improvised. `components/ui/` has no `Select` today, and while
`DropdownMenuRadioGroup` would carry the content (`PreferenceMenu` uses it for
three persisted preferences), a persisted configuration fact is a form choice
and deserves `combobox` semantics rather than `menuitemradio`.

It says everything before the click:

```
  MSCI World · CW8          depuis 2009    déjà téléchargé
  S&P 500 · CSPX            depuis 2010    à reconstituer
  Euro Stoxx 50 · MSE       depuis 2008    à reconstituer
  CAC 40 · C40              depuis 2008    à reconstituer
  MSCI Emerging · AEEM      depuis 2010    à reconstituer
  ── Éligibles au PEA ──
  MSCI World · WPEA         depuis 2024    à reconstituer
  S&P 500 · PE500           depuis 2019    à reconstituer
```

> *Ces fonds sont éligibles au PEA. Cela ne change rien au calcul : la
> comparaison rejoue vos flux dans vos propres comptes.*

And under the control, from the start:

> *La première comparaison reconstitue l'historique du fonds. Comptez quelques
> minutes ; la page se remplira toute seule.*

Three reasons each column earns its place:

- **the inception date** makes truncation visible *before* the choice. Without
  it, `WPEA.PA` silently costs an owner started in 2013 eleven years of their
  own history, discovered after the fact.
- **the download state** is what finally makes #760's own rule useful — the
  ticket keeps up to seven consulted series *precisely so switching back is
  instant*, and without a marker every switch is a coin-flip between instant and
  two hours.
- **the PEA group** defuses a label the ticket itself says enters no
  computation. Ungrouped and unexplained, it reads as advice, and it points at
  the worst available answer.

Names are **by index, ticker subordinate**. The legend names the index too,
never the ticker.

## 5. The gross/net toggle

`Segmented mode="pressed"` — two renderings of one slot, which is
`Segmented.tsx:41-44`'s exact semantic. Labels `Brut` / `Net d'impôt`. Screen
state, never persisted, per #760.

**The verdict is the gross one.** #760 says the toggle answers *how much is
left*, not *who wins*; so the untoggled state is the verdict.

**The inversion line is conditional and silent otherwise.** It is rendered only
when the two signs actually differ — both sides computed, signs compared — not
on the weaker predicate *the accounts have different rates*, which is true of
almost every French owner (PEA + CTO + assurance-vie) and would therefore be a
permanent warning nobody reads.

| | French | English source |
|---|---|---|
| ahead when net | `Net d'impôt, le classement s'inverse : vous passez devant.` | `After tax, the ranking flips: you come out ahead.` |
| behind when net | `Net d'impôt, le classement s'inverse : vous passez derrière.` | `After tax, the ranking flips: you come out behind.` |

This follows `receipts.ts`'s idiom: *"Zero duplicates says nothing, out loud."*

**The unit of computation is per account, from the first version.**
`taxation_projection.py:221` takes the floor at zero **per account** — *"a loss
owes nothing, and it does not owe a negative for another account to absorb"* —
so the net total is `Σ (1 − rᵢ) · max(gᵢ, 0)` and never `(1 − r̄) · g`. Computed
on an aggregate it is wrong the moment one account loses while another gains,
which is the nominal case. This reverses #760's stated order: the per-account
unit is #983's, and #760 needs it first. #983 then inherits it instead of
introducing it, which is what makes #983 *"the same function called three times"*
as its own ticket promises.

## 6. No range control

The screen **announces** the period it covers and does not let anyone choose
one. #760 already writes the first half; the second half is stated here so
nobody copies the dashboard's range row by reflex.

A counterfactual replays the flows from the beginning. Narrowing it to YTD does
not shorten the answer, it changes the question — it needs a common starting
point on 1 January on both sides, which is a different computation — and
narrowing it to 1M measures mostly the last session's noise.

## 7. The state table

Eight renderings, of which six are absences, plus two things that are **not**
absences and one deliberate silence. Every one maps to a primitive that already
exists.

| State | Rendering |
|---|---|
| no base currency declared | `<NoBaseCurrency/>`, the gate of `DashboardPage.tsx:247` |
| no events in the ledger | `EmptyState` + a link to `Grand livre` |
| no reference chosen | `EmptyState`, whose one `action` is the selector itself |
| the fund's backfill is **not terminal** | the rebuild panel of §8 — **the whole screen**, head included |
| the fund stopped answering | `absence.noQuote` — *« N relevés consécutifs, aucun cours »*, a count and never a verdict |
| the staleness ceiling fired, `orphan_symbols` took the series back | back to *no reference chosen*. A different screen, not a different sentence |
| a daily FX rate is missing | the *awaiting rate* rendering of `absence.ts` |
| the read did not answer | `<Unreadable/>` — the page's failure, not the reference's |
| the write was refused | `<Refusal/>`, mounted beside the selector. Choosing a reference **is a write** |
| in flight | nothing at all, title included. `readonly X[] \| null`, never `[]` |
| truncated history | **not an absence**: a caption on a present figure, see §9 |
| a receipt after the choice | **none.** The screen changing under the gesture is the acknowledgement |

**The load-bearing rule: while the reference's backfill is not terminal, no
figure at all.** `absence.ts:30` — *"the arithmetic is decided by terminality,
and by nothing else."* A gap computed over a half-built series is a **wrong**
number, not a short one: −38 % that silently repairs itself an hour later, seen
by the owner and by nobody else. This is the most likely shipping bug of the
whole ticket.

**How the front learns terminality.** *(Corrected 2026-09-20 by the eng review:
the conclusion below stands, the reasoning that reached it did not.)* #982's
acceptance criterion 4 is about the **valuation call site**, not about the
function: it forbids the index from reaching the day axis of the portfolio's
own curve. Calling `terminal_symbols(store, {reference: window}, now)` on the
comparison route is a different call site asking a different question — *has
this fund's own backfill finished* — and it is the only durable source of the
answer. `benchmark_view` does exactly that, and a test holds the valuation
curve to the ledger's symbols with a reference named and quoted in the store.

What it still cannot come from is `/api/runtime`: `absence.ts:52-60` already
refused exactly that for positions, because a terminality inherited from an
**optional** diagnostic read turns every line non-terminal the moment that
probe falls over. Nor from `runtime_state.Recorder`, which the eng review added
and which is worse: a per-process dict wiped on restart, so the figure would
appear after one deploy and vanish on the next.

So it rides on the comparison's own payload, beside the figure it governs:

```
  { reference, terminal, covered_from, covered_to,
    gap_gross, gap_net, points_written, points_expected, … }
```

One contract: the figure and the right to display it arrive together, so they
cannot disagree between two reads. Its cost is named: by
`mcp-list-positions-est-lapi-web`, publishing a member on the MCP surface
publishes it in the web API contract at the same instant. There is no MCP-only
publication.

**Currency.** The seven funds are EUR-priced. A non-EUR base currency converts
the fund's series day by day through `fx._daily_rate`, like everything else in
the product — that is the honest answer, since an owner who had actually bought
`CW8.PA` would have borne the same FX. A day with no rate puts the screen in the
*awaiting rate* state above.

## 8. The first run, which is the nominal path

The arc as #760 specified it: the reader clicks an entry promising a comparison
→ empty state → picks among seven tickers → a configuration fact is written,
`tracked_symbols` widens, a brand-new symbol backfills from 2009 in
`backfill_chunk_days` chunks spaced `backfill_interval` apart — **minutes to
hours** → the screen they just paid a choice for has nothing.

| Step | The reader does | Feels | What holds it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | lands on `Référence` | curious, slightly wary of a form | the empty state names the one thing to do and its `action` **is** the selector |
| 2 | opens the selector | evaluating | inception date, download state, PEA group defused (§4) |
| 3 | reads the hint under it | forewarned | *« Comptez quelques minutes ; la page se remplira toute seule. »* |
| 4 | chooses | committed | the screen changes under the gesture — that is the receipt |
| 5 | **waits** | this is where it breaks without §8 | the rebuild panel below |
| 6 | comes back | expectant | the head is filled, the reference named in the eyebrow |
| 7 | reads a gap against them | judged, with no recourse | the `Explain` bubble states the limit; #983's slot is reserved |
| 8 | switches reference | deciding, not gambling | *déjà téléchargé* vs *à reconstituer* |

**Step 5 needs a narrow, written exception to #829.** `DashboardPage.tsx:15-18`
posts *"No installation fact lands here"* and sends reconstruction progress to
the notifications panel and only there. Applied literally, day 1 ends on a dead
screen. #829's two arguments do not reach this page: the fact is not invisible
to whoever landed elsewhere (it was **started by a gesture on this page**), and
it does not compete with the bell (the bell does not carry it — an index
backfill is a workload `notifications.ts` does not know). **The fact has zero
announcers, not two.**

So, in the slot the figure will occupy:

> *Reconstitution de l'historique de CW8.PA.*
> *Remonté jusqu'au 4 mars 2014 sur 16 années à couvrir.*
> `[████████░░░░░░░░]`

**Not `RebuildBlock`.** That block measures
`(horizon → today) / (first event → today)` and names *which account is holding
it back*; both ends and the name are meaningless for a fund. The bounds here are
the fund's own inception and today, which the backfill already knows. **No
promised duration** — `RebuildBlock` already refuses to promise an hour, and a
mute symbol backing off to twenty-four hours is why.

## 9. Truncation is a caption, not an absence

With `WPEA.PA` from 2024-04-02 the screen has a complete, correct answer over a
shorter window. Nothing is missing. `absence.ts:4` — *"the em dash means there
is nothing to compute; anything merely missing is named instead"* — and here
there **is** something to compute, so neither rendering applies. Making it a
fifth rendering of absence is the fifth that `absence.ts:19` explicitly refuses,
and rendered as an `EmptyState` it would erase the data it qualifies.

It is a permanent muted line under the verdict, which states the covered period
always, and states what truncation costs when it bites:

> *Comparé depuis le 2 avril 2024, premier cours de ce fonds. Vos 11 années
> antérieures ne sont pas dans cet écart.*

**The caption reads the period the computation honoured, never the fund's
inception alone.** *(Corrected 2026-09-20 by the eng review.)* Two things move
it that this section did not account for: the seed lands at the **later** of
the fund's first close and the account's own first written day, so an owner who
started after the fund did is truncated by their own history and not by the
fund's; and the period can **end early** — a withdrawal the reference could not
have funded closes it that day, and so does a close with no exchange rate. A
caption naming a period the arithmetic did not run over is worse than none,
which is why the payload publishes `covered_from`, `covered_to` and `ended`
rather than letting the screen derive them from `inception`.

## 10. The chart

Two curves in euros, and **the area between them is the gap** — the same reading
`PortfolioChart` already gives *Montants*, where *"the area between the two **is**
the gain"*. That makes the drawing literally the head figure. No base-100
normalisation: a third convention on a screen already carrying two.

Everything else is `PortfolioChart`'s settled behaviour, reused rather than
re-argued: the x domain is the data's and never the window asked for, the value
axis is floored at zero when nothing drawn is negative, `ChartTooltip` answers
the pointer in the reader's language and reporting currency, and the legend
names curves and states no rules.

## 11. 390 px

Under 768 px the sidebar is a drawer (`use-mobile.ts`), leaving ~350 px.

- **head** — same `Card`, terms in one column. `Head.tsx` already does this at
  `grid-cols-1`.
- **selector** — full width, trigger ≥ 44 px tall. Inside the menu, each entry
  goes to two lines: name + ticker on the first, inception date and download
  state muted on the second.
- **toggle** — full width, two equal halves, ≥ 44 px. A compact `Segmented`
  falls to 32 px, which is under the touch minimum.
- **chart** — kept, shorter. Seventeen years on 350 px does not read to the day,
  but *the gap is widening* and *the gap is closing* read perfectly well, and
  that is half the information. No other screen in this product removes content
  by width; this one will not be the first.

The control row uses the same `flex-wrap` + `justify-end` fold the dashboard's
control row already uses. Nothing new.

## 12. Accessibility

- **selector** — accessible name `Référence de comparaison` (`Comparison
  benchmark`), current value announced by the component.
- **toggle** — `Segmented mode="pressed"`, so `aria-pressed`: it swaps what the
  slot below draws. Not a radiogroup — that would name it a setting, and the
  page has no other setting for it to be one of.
- **chart** — `aria-hidden`, legend readable. This is honest here and only here:
  §2 puts the whole answer in the head, in prose and in figures, so a non-visual
  reader loses nothing but the shape.
- **the head carries `aria-live="polite"`**, so switching from `CW8` to `C40`
  reads the eyebrow, the figure and the verdict back. Same reasoning as
  `Refusal`, which keeps `role="status"` because *"the reader is already looking
  at the control they pressed"*: this is the result of a gesture, not an ambient
  state. Polite and not assertive, so it never interrupts a reading in progress.

> **Flagged, out of scope.** No chart in this product has any non-visual
> reading: the only `aria-` attributes in `PortfolioChart` are `aria-hidden` on
> legend swatches. That is a product-wide gap and deserves its own ticket, not a
> clause in this one.

## 13. The reference does not leave its screen

No third curve on the dashboard chart, no mention on the share sheet. The
dashboard answers *where am I*, this page answers *against what*. A reference
curve without its verdict is a gap the reader eyeballs, which is exactly what
this feature exists to replace — and the dashboard would inherit every state of
§7 to render.

## NOT in scope

- **Several references at once.** #760's call, unchanged: three references turn
  a verdict into a table, and the head figure loses its subject.
- **A free symbol field.** #760's call, unchanged.
- **A 60/40 basket.** Not a quoted symbol — a rebalancing mechanism of its own.
- **A hidden data table for the two series.** ~4 300 rows per curve is
  compliance without use, and it would fix a product-wide gap on one page.
- **Bringing #983 forward into this PR.** A single PR carrying a new route, a
  new primitive, a new endpoint *and* the decomposition is a PR nobody really
  reviews. Its slot is reserved instead.
- **A motion system.** The product has none; this screen will not invent one.
- **What *the same period* means when the portfolio was fully sold and
  re-bought.** A computation-semantics question, not a screen one. Belongs to
  `/plan-eng-review`.
